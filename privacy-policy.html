Privacy Policy

The short version

JSON Desk doesn't collect, track or sell any data about you. There's no account and no analytics.
Everything you create is stored on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox.
The app goes online only when you send a request from the API Client, and only to the address you entered.
If you email for support, your message is used to help you and nothing else.

Who we are

JSON Desk is made by Ashok Arbad, an independent developer based in Pune, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the JSON Desk app for macOS, including its menu bar icon and its Shortcuts and Spotlight actions, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing, through the app. JSON Desk has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own, and no third-party libraries or SDKs. We don't run a server that receives your documents, settings or usage.

What the app stores on your Mac

To work, JSON Desk saves the following on your Mac, inside its macOS sandbox container. None of it is sent to us.

WhatWhere and how
Your libraryDocuments, projects, folders, tags, requests, schemas, JSONPath queries and Recently Deleted items, in a single file named store.json.
API Client saved APIs and environmentsSaved APIs, their folders, and environments with their variable values, in the app's preferences (macOS user defaults).
Settings and recent tool inputsYour settings, and the last values you entered on the Tools screens, in the app's preferences.
A recovery copy, only if neededIf the library file can't be read, the app saves a copy named store.unreadable-<timestamp>.json before starting a fresh library, so nothing is silently overwritten.
JWT secretsNot stored. The secret you type to verify or sign a token is kept in memory and never written to disk.

This data is stored as plain, unencrypted text. It is protected by your macOS user account, by FileVault if you have it turned on, and by the app sandbox. Locking a document in JSON Desk makes it read-only; it does not encrypt it.

About tokens and keys. If you put an API key or token in a request header or an environment variable, it is saved as plain text in the app's preferences, and in any backup or Postman collection you export. Avoid saving live production secrets in JSON Desk.

Network connections

JSON Desk connects to the internet in one situation: when you send a request from the API Client. The request goes directly from your Mac to the address you entered, with the headers and body you wrote and with your environment's variables filled in. It does not pass through any server of ours.

The server you contact receives that request and your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies to what it does with them. Only send requests to services you trust and are allowed to use.

Importing cURL commands, Postman collections and OpenAPI or Swagger files happens on your Mac. The app does not fetch anything when it imports them.

The clipboard and the menu bar

The menu bar icon reads your clipboard only when you choose one of its actions, such as Format or Validate. Formatting and minifying put the result back on your clipboard. The clipboard's contents are processed on your Mac and are not sent anywhere or stored by the app.

Shortcuts and Spotlight

JSON Desk's actions for formatting, validating and converting JSON run on your Mac and only process the input you or your shortcut give them. Shortcuts and Spotlight are part of macOS, and Apple's privacy policy covers how they work.

Files, export and sharing

JSON Desk asks macOS for access to a file only when you choose it: when you drop a file on an editor, import a backup, collection or OpenAPI file, or export a backup or output. The app can read or write only the files you pick.

When you share an output, for example by AirDrop or Mail, macOS sends it to the destination you choose. We never receive it. A backup contains your whole library, so store and share it with the same care as the original.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual items in the app. They move to Recently Deleted for the period you set, then are removed for good.
  • Use Settings › Clear all data to erase everything the app has saved.
  • Delete the app and its folder in ~/Library/Containers to remove every trace of it from your Mac.

Apple and the App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates of JSON Desk through the Mac App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have chosen in macOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymized crash reports and usage statistics for JSON Desk. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include, such as screenshots or sample JSON. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem you reported. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message, and we delete support emails once they're no longer needed. Please remove passwords, tokens and personal data from any JSON you send.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also connects to Google, which receives your IP address under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

JSON Desk is a developer tool and isn't directed at children. Because the app doesn't collect personal data, we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU and UK GDPR give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The only personal data we could hold is a support email you sent us. To see it or have it deleted, write to the address below and we'll act on your request within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how JSON Desk handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change is released. If a future version ever collects data, it will be described here first.
